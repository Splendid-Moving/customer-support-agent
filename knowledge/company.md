## Where we work

Most of our work is local moving around Los Angeles and the surrounding area —
Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena, Long Beach, and the Valley.

We also do long-distance and out-of-state moves, but those are priced
individually rather than by the hour, so a manager handles them directly.

## What we do

- Local residential moves — apartments, condos, houses
- Commercial and office moves
- Packing and unpacking
- Furniture disassembly and reassembly
- Labor-only jobs (loading or unloading, no truck)

## Rates

All rates are hourly, with a **3 hour minimum charge**.

| Crew | Rate | Typically suits |
|---|---|---|
| 2 movers + truck | $115 – $125 / hour | Studio or 1 bedroom apartment |
| 3 movers + truck | $145 – $155 / hour | 2 bedroom apartment or small office |
| 4 movers + truck | $180 – $190 / hour | 3+ bedrooms or a medium office |

The exact rate within each range depends on the day and the details of the job.
A manager confirms it when they put the estimate together.

There are no hidden fees. What we quote is what gets billed.

## What's included in the hourly rate

- A 26ft moving truck
- Shrink wrap, tape and packing paper at no extra charge
- Furniture blankets and wardrobe boxes to use during the move, no extra charge
- Disassembly and reassembly of furniture
- A uniformed, professional crew

## Deposit

New bookings take a $50 deposit to hold the date. It comes off the final bill.

## Estimates

Estimates are free. We put one together from a list of your items or from photos
of what needs to move — the more we can see, the closer the estimate lands.
