# About us

## Who we are

We're a moving company based in Los Angeles. We're fully licensed and carry
business liability insurance, and we can provide a certificate of insurance on
request — buildings often ask for one before move day.

That's coverage on us as a company, and it is a different thing from the
coverage on a customer's belongings during the move. For what happens if
something of theirs is damaged, use the released-value protection answer in the
policies — never say a customer's items are "fully insured".

We're rated 4.9 stars on both Google and Yelp.

## Hours

We're open every day, 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

## Where we work

Most of our work is local moving around Los Angeles and the surrounding area —
Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena, Long Beach, and the Valley.

We also do long-distance and out-of-state moves.

## What we do

- Local residential moves — apartments, condos, houses
- Commercial and office moves
- Packing and unpacking
- Furniture disassembly and reassembly
- Labor-only jobs, where we load or unload but don't provide the truck

## What comes with the crew

We bring a 26ft truck. Disassembly and reassembly of furniture is part of the
job, not an add-on.

## After you send us an estimate request

A manager goes through it and gets back to you, usually the same day. If you'd
rather not wait, calling the office is always faster.

## TVs

We can take a TV off the wall, wrap it, and transport it. We can't mount it at
the new place.
