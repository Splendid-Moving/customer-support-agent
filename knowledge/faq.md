# Common questions

## Do you take apart and reassemble furniture?

Yes, that's included. Beds, tables, anything that needs to come apart to get out
the door — we take it apart at the old place and put it back together at the new
one.

## Can you handle my TV?

We take TVs off the wall, wrap them, and transport them. We do **not** mount TVs
at the new place — that needs a different trade.

## Do I need to buy boxes and packing material?

Shrink wrap, tape and packing paper are included. So are furniture blankets and
wardrobe boxes, which we bring for the move and take away again afterwards. If
you want us to pack the whole place, we do that too — it's the same hourly rate,
it just takes longer.

## How long will my move take?

That depends on how much there is, the stairs or elevator at both ends, and how
far apart the two places are. It's exactly what an estimate is for — send over
photos or a list of items and a manager works out a realistic window.

## What's the minimum charge?

Three hours, on every job.

## Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, fully. We can send a certificate of insurance if your building needs one.

## How far in advance should I book?

Sooner is better, especially for weekends and the end of the month, which fill up
first. We do take short-notice jobs when we have a crew free.

## Do you move on weekends and holidays?

We work every day, 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

## Do you do long-distance or out-of-state moves?

We do. Those aren't billed hourly like local moves, so a manager prices each one
individually and gets in touch directly.

## Can you move a piano, a safe, or a pool table?

Sometimes — it depends on the item, the weight, and the access at both ends. A
manager needs to look at photos before we commit to it.

## Do you store furniture?

We don't run our own storage. We can move things into a storage unit you've
arranged.

## What payment do you take?

A manager confirms payment options when they set up the booking.

## Something got damaged — what do I do?

Call the office on (323) 645-2636 and ask for a manager. They handle claims
directly.
